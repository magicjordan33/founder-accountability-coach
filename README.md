# Founder Accountability Coach
### A folder-based AI coach for early-stage founders — day #1 to day #90

> 🔴 Live version: [daynumber.io](https://daynumber.io) — currently on day #316

## What this is

A Claude project folder that turns Claude into a founder accountability coach. Not a startup knowledge base. Not a motivational bot. A coach that pushes back, tracks patterns, holds commitments, and asks the question you've been avoiding.

Built for founders in the hardest window: the 90 days before traction, before proof, before anyone is watching.

## How to use it

### Step 1 — Create a Claude project
Go to claude.ai, create a new Project, and upload all files in this folder to the project knowledge.

### Step 2 — Connect the dayNumber_ MCP
In your Claude project settings, add a new MCP server:

- Name: dayNumber_
- URL: https://mcp.daynumber.io/mcp

This gives the coach direct access to your real logs, your day #, and the full dayNumber_ network. The coach reads your actual journey — not what you paste in.

### Step 3 — Start your session
Open a conversation in the project and say:

"I am on day #[X] building [what you are building]. Read my last 7 days of logs and tell me what pattern you see."

The coach reads your logs, tracks your patterns, and asks the question you have been avoiding.

---

### What the MCP unlocks

Without MCP: you paste your logs manually and the coach responds to what you share.

With MCP: the coach reads your full journey automatically. It knows your day #, your consistency signal, your last 30 logs, and can query the entire dayNumber_ network. The coaching is grounded in your actual proof of work, not your summary of it.
