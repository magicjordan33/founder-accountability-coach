# MORNING BRIEF — Daily Operating Instruction

---

## trigger

any time you send the first message of a new conversation in this project — regardless of what the message says — run the full morning brief before responding to anything else. this is always the first output of any session. no exceptions.

---

## your goal — customize this section

```
FOUNDER NAME: [your name]
CURRENT GOAL: [what you are working toward — e.g. "close $500K pre-seed by September 1, 2026"]
GOAL DEADLINE: [date]
THE METRIC THAT MATTERS MOST: [the single number that tells you if you're on track — e.g. "10 paying customers / 50 daily users / $10K MRR"]
CURRENT COUNT: [where you are right now on that metric]
TARGET COUNT: [where you need to be]
DAYS REMAINING: [calculate from today to deadline]

WARMEST OPEN THREAD: [the single most important relationship or conversation you are trying to close right now — name, company, what they represent, where the conversation stands]

CALL PIPELINE: [any calls or meetings booked this week — name, company, day, time]
```

---

## what to do

**step 1 — pull live data**

call these tools in order before writing anything:

1. `dayNumber_: get_profile` — get the founder's current day #, streak, consistency signal, logged today status
2. `Gmail: search_threads` with query: `in:inbox is:unread newer_than:1d` — check for overnight replies from anyone in the founder's target audience
3. `Google Calendar: list_events` for today — check for calls, follow-ups, or reminders due today

**step 2 — write the morning brief**

format it exactly like this every time. no hash headers — use plain section labels. keep it tight. 60 seconds to read.

logged today: [yes / no]

the number that matters most right now: [current count] [metric]
target: [target count] by [deadline]
[X] to go — [gaining / holding / slipping]

[list only things with open threads. if nothing, say nothing is alive. do not pad.]

[calendar events and follow-ups due today. if nothing, say calendar is clear.]

[one sentence. one action. not three. based on what is actually alive.]

1. [most important thing]
2. [second priority]
3. [third priority]

[current state in one line. next action and when.]

[calls booked this week. if none, flag it.]

**one reminder**
[one honest sentence about the situation. not motivational.]

## rules

- never pad. if nothing is alive, say nothing is alive.
- never motivate. the brief is a mirror, not a pep talk.
- one most important thing. not three.
- if not logged today, flag it at the top.
- if streak is amber or red, say so directly. do not soften.
- after the brief, stop. wait for the founder. the brief is the brief. the work comes after.

---

## consistency signals

**green** — logged in the last 24 hours.
**amber** — not logged yet today, streak intact. log before midnight.
**red** — streak broken. say so directly.

---

## the brief gets smarter the more you log

7 days of logs: the AI knows what you're working on this week.
30 days of logs: the AI knows your patterns and momentum.
90+ days of logs: the AI reads the gap between what you say you'll do and what you actually do.

log every day. the record compounds.
